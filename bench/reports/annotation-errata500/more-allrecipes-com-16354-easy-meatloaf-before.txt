 Get back to basics with this easy meatloaf recipe. Made with simple ingredients, it comes together quickly, and tastes delicious. Perfect for busy weeknights and sure to please everyone at your table.






  Meatloaf Ingredients  


 These are the simple ingredients you'll need to make this homemade meatloaf recipe:






 · For the loaf: ground beef, an egg, an onion, milk, bread crumbs, salt, and pepper

· For the sauce: ketchup, brown sugar, mustard






  What Temperature Is Meatloaf Done?  


 Use an instant read thermometer, inserted into the middle, to check the doneness of the meatloaf. It's completely cooked and safe to eat at 160 degrees F.






  What Goes With Meatloaf?  


 In need of some recipe ideas to serve with meatloaf for classic comfort food? We've got you covered! Explore our collection of 12 Best Side Dishes for Meatloaf for delicious inspiration. Here's a preview of what you'll find:

  How to Store and Freeze Meatloaf  


 Tightly wrap the meatloaf in plastic wrap or aluminum foil (or place it in an airtight container). Store meatloaf in the refrigerator for three to four days.To freeze meatloaf, wrap it in at least one layer of storage wrap followed by at least one layer of foil. For extra protection, place the double-wrapped meatloaf in a zip-top freezer bag labeled with the date. Store meatloaf in the freezer for three to four months.

1 ½ pounds ground beef






1 large egg






1  onion, chopped






1 cup milk






1 cup dried bread crumbs






  salt and pepper to taste






⅓ cup ketchup






2 tablespoons brown sugar






2 tablespoons prepared mustard








Directions






 Gather the ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Lightly grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.


 






 

 
Grant Webster / Food Styling: Lauren McAnelly / Prop Styling: Gabriel Greco










 Combine ground beef, onion, milk, bread crumbs, and egg in a large bowl, and season with salt and pepper. Press mixture evenly into the prepared loaf pan.


 






 

 
Grant Webster / Food Styling: Lauren McAnelly / Prop Styling: Gabriel Greco










 Stir ketchup, brown sugar, and mustard together in a small bowl until smooth, and spread evenly over the meatloaf.


 






 

 
Grant Webster / Food Styling: Lauren McAnelly / Prop Styling: Gabriel Greco










 Bake in the preheated oven until no longer pink in the center, about 1 hour. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the middle reads at least 160 degrees F (70 degrees C).


 






 

 
Grant Webster / Food Styling: Lauren McAnelly / Prop Styling: Gabriel Greco










 Serve hot and enjoy!


 






 

 
Grant Webster / Food Styling: Lauren McAnelly / Prop Styling: Gabriel Greco









 
Cook’s Note



You can use crushed crackers instead of bread crumbs for added flavor and texture.
